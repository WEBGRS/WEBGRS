CS student at UW–Madison. Portfolio: [webgrs.github.io](https://webgrs.github.io)

**Live tools**

- [UW Course Lookup](https://courses.madisonstudents.com/): grades and instructor GPA for every Fall 2026 and Spring 2027 course, plus Spring 2027 seats and a timetable planner
- [Madison Eats](https://food.madisonstudents.com/): every place to eat in Madison on one map, scored from six rating sources
- [Madison Rentals](https://housing.madisonstudents.com/): rentals near campus with per-person or whole-unit prices, floor plans and bus stops
- [Madison Essentials](https://guide.madisonstudents.com/): groceries, clinics, campus offices and other everyday places around campus, explained in English and Chinese
- [UW Digest](https://news.madisonstudents.com/): daily UW–Madison news ranked by importance, summarized in English or Chinese, also sent by email
- [Route Animator](https://webgrs.github.io/route-animator/): travel-route videos on a satellite globe, no account, no watermark
- [DataMap](https://webgrs.github.io/datamap/): paste regions and values, get a choropleth map

**Chrome extensions:** [Top Hat Watch](https://github.com/WEBGRS/tophat-watch) · [Geo Spoof](https://github.com/WEBGRS/geo-spoof)
