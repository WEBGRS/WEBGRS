CS student at UW–Madison. Portfolio: [webgrs.github.io](https://webgrs.github.io)

**Live tools**

- [UW Course Lookup](https://webgrs.github.io/uw-course-lookup/): grades, instructor GPA and seats for every Fall 2026 course, plus a timetable planner
- [Madison Eats](https://webgrs.github.io/madison-food-map/): every place to eat in Madison on one map, scored from six rating sources
- [Isthmus Rentals](https://webgrs.github.io/madison-rentals/): rentals near campus with per-person or whole-unit prices, floor plans and bus stops
- [Route Animator](https://webgrs.github.io/route-animator/): travel-route videos on a satellite globe, no account, no watermark
- [DataMap](https://webgrs.github.io/datamap/): paste regions and values, get a choropleth map

**Chrome extensions:** [Top Hat Watch](https://github.com/WEBGRS/tophat-watch) · [Geo Spoof](https://github.com/WEBGRS/geo-spoof)
